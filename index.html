Hidden Forest Animals

1. Introduction

CodeS Dev Lab ("we," "us," or "our") develops and provides Hidden Forest Animals (the "App"), a relaxing forest-themed logic puzzle game. Players solve puzzles to find animals hidden beneath leaves, choose from different animal themes, and enjoy level-based and daily puzzle challenges.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information and what choices you have when using it.

2. Personal Information Collection and Use

Hidden Forest Animals does not collect, transmit, or share personal information about its users. We do not ask users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal details to play the game.

We do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

3. Android Permissions

The App does not request Android system permissions. Its core puzzle gameplay does not require access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or personal files.

4. Game Progress and Preferences

The App may save non-personal gameplay information locally on your device, such as level progress, selected animal, game settings, or puzzle status, to support your experience. This information is not transmitted to us or stored on our servers.

You can generally remove locally stored game information by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently reset your progress and preferences.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. The App does not send personal information to CodeS Dev Lab for processing.

Services independently provided by your device operating system or app marketplace, such as Google Play, may process information under their own privacy policies. Such processing is separate from the App's own data-handling practices.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or maintain personal information on our servers, we have no server-side personal information to retain or delete. Any locally stored gameplay information remains on your device until it is removed through the App or your device's storage-management or uninstall options.

For privacy questions or requests, you may contact us using the email address below.

7. Security

We design the App to work without collecting personal information. Locally stored gameplay data is handled by the device's application storage mechanisms. Although no system can guarantee absolute security, limiting personal-data collection helps reduce privacy risks.

8. Children's Privacy

Hidden Forest Animals contains general-audience puzzle content that may be enjoyed by people of different ages. However, the App is not specifically directed toward children, and we do not intentionally collect personal information from children or any other users.

If you believe the App has collected personal information unexpectedly, please contact us so we can investigate.

9. International Users

The App is available to users in different regions. As described above, we do not collect or transfer personal information through the App.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, applicable requirements, or our information practices. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update its effective date. If future versions introduce features that change how user data is handled, we will revise this policy as appropriate.
